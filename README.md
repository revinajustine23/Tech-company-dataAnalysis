# Tech Company Data Analysis & Relational Database

##  Project Overview
This project presents an end-to-end data analytics workflow for a fictional **Tech Company**. It covers relational database schema design, advanced multi-table SQL queries, Excel data cleaning, and an interactive Power BI dashboard for business insights.

---

## Tools & Technologies
* **Database & SQL:** MySQL / MariaDB (Schema Architecture, Constraints, Multi-table JOINs)
* **Data Preparation:** Microsoft Excel (Cleaning & Data Transformation)
* **Visualization:** Power BI Interactive Dashboard

---

##  Key Features & Deliverables
1. **Relational Schema Design:** Structured tables for employees, departments, and project assignments.
2. **SQL Analytics:** Complex queries evaluating sales performance, employee allocation, and revenue metrics.
3. **Data Cleaning:** Handled missing values, standardized formats, and deduplicated records in Excel.
4. **Power BI Dashboard:** Executive visuals displaying regional sales metrics and performance breakdown.

---
### 📊 Product Sales Performance & Revenue Analysis

Through the Power BI dashboard developed for Tech Company, I performed a deep-dive analysis into product category revenues to identify purchasing trends and top-performing merchandise:

* **Top-Performing Products (High Sales):** The **Computers** category overwhelmingly leads the market, generating the highest total revenue of **5.7K**. This indicates strong customer demand and serves as the primary revenue driver for the business. It is closely followed by **Accessories (1.6K)** and **Displays (1.4K)** as steady secondary revenue streams.
* **Low-Performing Products (Low Sales):** Conversely, the **Storage** category recorded the lowest performance, bringing in **0.4K** in revenue. This insight highlights an opportunity for management to either bundle storage products with high-selling computers or revise pricing strategies to boost its market uptake.

### 🌍 Customer Geographic Distribution

This pie chart illustrates the regional breakdown of our customer base across major cities, highlighting key market concentrations:

* **Top Markets:** **Arusha** leads with **14 customers (28%)**, closely followed by **Kilimanjaro** with **13 customers (26%)**, making northern Tanzania the primary hub for business activity.
* **Expanding Regions:** Steady engagement is observed in **Dar es Salaam (9 customers / 18%)** and **Zanzibar (7 customers / 14%)**.
* **Emerging Markets:** Regional reach extends to **Dodoma (4 customers / 8%)** and **Mwanza (3 customers / 6%)**, presenting future growth opportunities.
